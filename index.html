PRODUCT LINE

Chemistries we blend and supply

A full slate of oilfield intermediates, formulated and adjusted to the water, crude, and conditions of each site.

01 · CI

Corrosion Inhibitors

Protect pipe and equipment from the internal corrosion that shortens the life of a well.

02 · SI

Scale Inhibitors

Keep mineral scale from building up in tubing, flowlines, and downhole equipment.

03 · PI

Paraffin Inhibitors

Prevent wax deposition that slows flow and clogs production equipment.

04 · SF

Surfactants

Reduce interfacial tension to improve flow and clean-up across production stages.

05 · WC

Water Clarifiers

Speed the separation of solids and oil from produced water.

06 · HS

H₂S Scavengers

Neutralize hydrogen sulfide for safer, more compliant operations.

07 · EB

Emulsion Breakers

Split oil-water emulsions quickly and cleanly at the treater.

08 · DG

Degreasers

Cut through crude and grease buildup on equipment and surfaces.

09 · BC

Registered Biocides

Control bacterial growth that drives souring and microbial corrosion.

10 · SB

Specialty Blends

Custom formulations built around the chemistry of a specific site.

Toll Blending

Contract blending capacity for partners who need volume without the overhead.
